# Product Mission

## Pitch

**Learning Hungarian** is a free, interactive educational website that helps English-speaking travelers and expats learn Hungarian from complete beginner to B2 proficiency by providing a comprehensive 52-week curriculum with immediate feedback exercises, built on proven polyglot methodology and second language acquisition research.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Travelers to Hungary**: Tourists planning trips who want practical language skills for navigation, ordering food, and basic interactions
- **Expats and Relocators**: Professionals and families moving to Hungary who need functional language skills for daily life and integration
- **Heritage Learners**: People with Hungarian family connections wanting to connect with their roots
- **Language Enthusiasts**: Polyglots and hobbyists attracted to Hungarian's unique linguistic structure

### User Personas

**Sarah** (28-35)
- **Role:** Digital nomad / remote worker
- **Context:** Planning a 3-month stay in Budapest, wants to move beyond tourist interactions
- **Pain Points:** Most Hungarian resources assume prior language learning experience; existing apps lack depth for serious study; can't find structured progression
- **Goals:** Achieve conversational ability to make local friends, navigate bureaucracy, and feel integrated

**Michael** (40-50)
- **Role:** Corporate transferee with family
- **Context:** Relocating to Hungary for 2-year work assignment, needs practical survival skills quickly
- **Pain Points:** Paid courses are expensive and time-inflexible; Hungarian grammar seems impenetrable; needs to learn alongside family responsibilities
- **Goals:** Handle daily tasks (shopping, appointments, school communication) independently within 6 months

**Emma** (22-28)
- **Role:** University graduate, language enthusiast
- **Context:** Fascinated by Hungarian's unique structure (agglutination, vowel harmony), wants systematic learning
- **Pain Points:** Free resources are scattered and incomplete; wants to understand the "why" behind grammar rules
- **Goals:** Achieve solid B2 proficiency and understand Hungarian linguistics deeply

## The Problem

### Hungarian is Exceptionally Difficult for English Speakers

Hungarian ranks among the most challenging languages for English speakers due to its unique Uralic origins, agglutinative structure, 18 grammatical cases, vowel harmony rules, and the definite/indefinite conjugation system. The US Foreign Service Institute estimates 1,100+ hours to achieve proficiency.

**Existing solutions fall short:**
- **Paid apps** (Duolingo, Babbel) offer shallow coverage and gamification over substance
- **Textbooks** lack interactivity and immediate feedback
- **Private tutors** are expensive and inaccessible
- **Free resources** are fragmented, inconsistent, and rarely progress beyond basics

**Our Solution:** A structured, comprehensive, free curriculum specifically designed for Hungarian's unique challenges, with interactive exercises providing immediate feedback at every step.

## Differentiators

### Hungarian-Specific Pedagogy

Unlike generic language apps that apply the same template to every language, Learning Hungarian is built specifically around Hungarian's unique challenges. Every lesson addresses vowel harmony, demonstrates case suffix patterns, and explicitly teaches the definite/indefinite conjugation distinction.

This results in learners understanding the systematic logic behind Hungarian grammar rather than memorizing disconnected rules.

### Comprehensive Free Resource

Unlike paid platforms that paywall advanced content, Learning Hungarian provides a complete A1-to-B2 curriculum (52 weeks) entirely free and open source. No premium tiers, no subscription fees, no ads.

This results in equitable access for all learners regardless of financial situation.

### Research-Based Methodology

Unlike casual language apps, our curriculum applies established SLA principles: comprehensible input (Krashen's i+1), spaced repetition, active recall over passive recognition, and contextual learning through realistic scenarios.

This results in more effective retention and faster progress toward actual communicative ability.

### Progressive Skill Building

Unlike reference materials that present grammar in isolation, each lesson explicitly builds on prior knowledge with clear prerequisites. The curriculum scaffolds from alphabet through B2 complex expression.

This results in learners developing systematic, interconnected knowledge rather than fragmented facts.

## Success Metrics

### Engagement Metrics

- **Lesson Completion Rate:** Percentage of users completing each lesson's exercises
- **Return Visitor Rate:** Users returning within 7 days to continue learning
- **Session Duration:** Average time spent per learning session
- **Curriculum Depth:** How far users progress through the 52-week curriculum

### Learning Outcome Metrics

- **Exercise Accuracy:** Percentage of correct answers on first attempt (target: 70-80% indicates appropriate difficulty)
- **Retention Rate:** Performance on review exercises after time gaps
- **Self-Assessment Progression:** User-reported confidence levels at milestones

### Growth Metrics

- **Monthly Active Users:** Unique visitors engaging with lessons
- **Geographic Reach:** Learners from different countries/regions
- **Community Engagement:** GitHub stars, forks, and contributions
- **Referral Traffic:** Users arriving via recommendations or search
