Lesson 30: Future Expressions (Jövő idő kifejezése)

Learn how to express future actions in Hungarian using the "fog" + infinitive construction, present tense for future meaning, and essential time expressions.

Learning Objectives

  • Use "fog" + infinitive to express future actions
  • Understand when to use present tense for future meaning
  • Master time expressions: holnap, jövő héten, majd, etc.
  • Form questions and negations in the future tense

Section 1: Introduction to Future in Hungarian

Hungarian expresses future in two main ways:

  1. fog + infinitive - The formal future tense (similar to English "will")
  2. Present tense + time expression - Very common in everyday speech

Using "fog" + infinitive:

Holnap dolgozni fogok.

Tomorrow I will work.

Using present tense:

Holnap dolgozom.

Tomorrow I work. (I will work tomorrow)

Note: In everyday Hungarian, using present tense with a future time expression is often more natural than the "fog" construction. The "fog" form is used for emphasis or formal situations.

Section 2: Conjugation of "fog"

The verb fog (will) is conjugated like a regular verb, and the main verb stays in the infinitive form (-ni).

Pronoun fog conjugation Example with "dolgozni" (to work) Translation
énfogokdolgozni fogokI will work
tefogszdolgozni fogszyou will work
ő/Önfogdolgozni foghe/she/you (formal) will work
mifogunkdolgozni fogunkwe will work
tifogtokdolgozni fogtokyou (pl) will work
ők/Önökfognakdolgozni fognakthey/you (formal pl) will work

Word Order: The infinitive usually comes before "fog" but can come after for emphasis:

  • Dolgozni fogok. = I will work. (neutral)
  • Fogok dolgozni. = I WILL work. (emphatic)

More Examples:

Tanulni fogok magyarul.

I will study Hungarian.

Utazni fogsz Magyarországra?

Will you travel to Hungary?

Ő meg fog érkezni holnap.

He/She will arrive tomorrow.

Vásárolni fogunk a piacon.

We will shop at the market.

The word "majd": This is a very common word in Hungarian that indicates future action. It softens statements and is often used with present tense:

  • Majd meglátjuk. = We'll see. (literally: later we see)
  • Majd holnap. = Maybe tomorrow. / Later, tomorrow.
  • Majd én megcsinálom. = I'll do it (eventually).

Section 4: Using Present Tense for Future

In Hungarian, present tense is frequently used to express future actions, especially with scheduled events, plans, and when a time expression makes the future meaning clear.

Scheduled Events:

A vonat indul 8-kor.

The train leaves/will leave at 8.

Personal Plans:

Holnap megyek a fodrászhoz.

Tomorrow I'm going/will go to the hairdresser.

Near Future:

Jövök egy perc múlva.

I'm coming in a minute.

Arrangements:

Este találkozunk a barátokkal.

This evening we're meeting friends.

When to use "fog" instead of present tense:

  • For predictions: Esni fog. (It will rain.)
  • For promises: Meg fogom csinálni! (I will do it!)
  • For formal situations
  • For emphasis or certainty

Section 5: Negation and Questions

Negation with "nem":

  • Nem fogok menni. = I will not go.
  • Nem fog esni. = It will not rain.
  • Holnap nem dolgozom. = Tomorrow I won't work.

Questions:

  • Fogsz jönni? = Will you come?
  • Mikor fogsz érkezni? = When will you arrive?
  • Holnap dolgozol? = Will you work tomorrow?

Lesson Summary
